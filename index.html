Selected Impact

Eight process improvements built at Clarity AI between 2023 and 2026. Same idea every time: automate the repetitive part, keep people on review and judgement.

Clarity AI · Month‑end close

A three‑day close

Redesigning the close from the first data extract to the CFO pack.

Challenge

Closing six entities took around 14 days, most of it spent extracting data and building working papers by hand.

Approach

Scheduled NetSuite exports feed standard working papers in Google Sheets that calculate, reconcile and flag variances automatically. Recurring entries such as accruals load through CSV imports instead of being keyed in.

Outcome

The month closes in three working days, with the team reviewing results instead of producing them.

Close cycle
14 → 3 days
Entities closed
6

Clarity AI · Accounts payable

Accounts payable with almost no manual entry

From the incoming invoice to the payment batch, with checks built in.

Challenge

Vendor invoices were keyed in, coded and paid by hand, and errors only showed up at payment.

Approach

Automated invoice capture into NetSuite with approval workflows, AI‑generated posting descriptions and batched weekly payments. Every bill is checked against its invoice before approval.

Outcome

Data entry turned into exception review, and payment errors became visible before release.

Process time
−70%
Saved per month
~40 h

Clarity AI · CFO & board reporting

The CFO pack in an hour

Monthly reporting for the CFO and the board, drafted from system data.

Challenge

Variance analysis and commentary for the CFO and the board were written line by line every month.

Approach

A Google Sheets pipeline uses AI to draft variance explanations from NetSuite data under set materiality rules, and surfaces the top drivers behind each balance sheet movement.

Outcome

Consistent, review‑ready reporting every month, in the style the CFO already expects.

CFO pack, from a full day
~1 h

Clarity AI · NetSuite

NetSuite that works for the team

Scripts, searches and exports that replace recurring manual work.

Challenge

Recurring reports and journal entries were rebuilt from NetSuite by hand every month.

Approach

Custom scripts, saved searches and scheduled exports, plus CSV imports for recurring entries, so data reaches each working paper without copy and paste.

Outcome

Recurring work runs on a schedule, and every figure comes straight from the system.

Saved per month
~25 h

Clarity AI · Acquisitions & mergers

Acquisitions and mergers, end to end

Due diligence, transaction close and integration, on top of day‑to‑day operations.

Challenge

Transactions needed a full diligence pack, a clean cut‑off at the transaction date and a fast integration.

Approach

Prepared process and controls documentation for the data room, delivered the transaction close and mapped systems and chart of accounts for integration.

Outcome

Diligence requests answered within days, a clean close at the transaction date and a smooth handover to the combined finance team.

Clarity AI · Tax compliance

A compliance calendar that keeps itself up to date

Every filing deadline in six jurisdictions, with its evidence.

Challenge

Tax and statutory deadlines lived in a shared calendar that had to be updated by hand, so status was often out of date.

Approach

Deadlines flow automatically into each person's task list, and an AI scheduled task syncs completed filings and their evidence back to the shared calendar.

Outcome

One reliable view of every filing, with evidence attached and no manual status updates.

Deadlines tracked
150+

Clarity AI · Multi‑language invoices

Invoices in any language, booked in English

German, Arabic and other invoices summarised for the ERP in seconds.

Challenge

Supplier invoices in other languages had to be translated and summarised by hand before booking.

Approach

A reusable AI workflow reads each invoice and returns a one‑line English description in the format the ERP expects.

Outcome

Consistent descriptions across entities and much faster booking, with the accountant only checking the result.

Per invoice, from 5 min
~30 s

Audit‑ and diligence‑ready documentation in hours, kept current as processes change.
